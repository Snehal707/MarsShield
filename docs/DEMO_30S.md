# MarsShield in 30 seconds (NASA Space Apps)

A short pitch video or live demo on https://marsshield.vercel.app. For the full 3-minute walkthrough, see [DEMO.md](DEMO.md).

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0–6 s | The Mars overview opens: global MOLA map with Gale, Jezero and Olympus Mons marked | "Mars has no magnetic field and a thin atmosphere, so cosmic rays reach the ground. MarsShield shows how much, region by region." |
| 6–14 s | Click **Show the comparison chart and table**. The dock opens: dose against pressure, three sites marked | "One published transport model, one climate model, the same assumptions everywhere. Olympus Mons has an eighth of Gale's air, yet only about 4 % less effective dose, because the composition of the radiation shifts." |
| 14–22 s | Open **Gale Crater**, then **Experiment**, then **Reveal**: white rings for Curiosity's RAD measurements over the model's blue dots | "At Gale we test against reality: 322 Curiosity rover stops. Terrain helps, and the app shows where it does not." |
| 22–30 s | Open **Jezero**, click the terrain: the location dose appears, labelled exploratory | "Where nothing has been measured, MarsShield says so: modeled, not validated, with every source one click away." |

**Rules for the narration:**
- Say "model estimate" for Jezero and Olympus Mons.
- Never say "safe" or "measured" for anything outside Gale.
